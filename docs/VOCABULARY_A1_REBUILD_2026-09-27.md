## Same word twice in a unit with slightly different meanings

Both kept: *můj* (my / my, masc), *tvůj*, *matka* (mother / mother, formal), *otec*, *velký*, *malý*, *hezký*, *dobrý*. One of each pair could go.

## How cards are released

A card is introduced after the lesson it belongs to, at most 8 per lesson (the v1.2 rule). Where a lesson had more, the rest moved to the next lesson of the unit, or are released with the unit.
