Delete your Czechify account

Deleting your account permanently removes your Czechify account and everything synced to it. The app clears the copy on your device as part of the same step.

Fastest way: open Czechify and go to Settings → Account & data → Delete account, then confirm. It takes effect immediately.

If you cannot open the app

Email email.czechify@gmail.com and ask for your account to be deleted.

If you linked an email address to the account, write from that address so we can verify the request. If you never linked one, your account is anonymous and we have no way to identify which account is yours — in that case, uninstalling the app removes the copy on your device, and the anonymous account is deleted automatically after 90 days without use.

Never include your password, an access token, or a recording in the message. None of them are needed to delete an account.

What gets deleted

  • Your Czechify account itself, and any linked email address
  • Synced lesson progress, scores, XP, streaks and badges
  • Your review schedule and any custom cards you created
  • Your record of consent choices
  • The local database on your device, and cached audio

Your chat history with the AI tutor lives only on your device and goes with the local data.

What may remain briefly

Our hosting providers keep operational backups and security logs for their own retention periods. Those are not accessible to us as ordinary records and roll off on their own schedule.

Want a copy first?

Deletion cannot be undone. If you want your data before you delete it, use Settings → Account & data → Export first — it gives you a file containing everything held in your cloud account.
