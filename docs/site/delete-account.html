Delete your Czechify account

Deleting your account removes your Supabase account and synchronized learning data. The app also clears local learner data as part of the same flow.

Fastest option: open Czechify and choose Settings → Account & data → Delete account. Confirm the deletion in the app to permanently remove the account.

If you cannot open the app

Contact the maintainer through the Czechify GitHub repository and request account deletion. Do not post passwords, access tokens, recordings, or other sensitive information in a public issue. If you have linked an email address, say that you are contacting us from the linked address so the request can be verified.

What is deleted

The deletion flow removes the Supabase Auth account and associated synced progress, badges, review state, and custom cards. Local app data and cached audio are removed by the app. Operational backups and security logs may remain for the retention periods of the service providers.

See the Czechify Privacy Policy for more information.
