Czechify Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Czechify is developed and operated by Mahesh Pathak. This page is the public version of the policy shown inside the app.

Data stored locally

The app stores curriculum content, learning progress, scores, streaks, review state, chat history, settings, and temporary audio locally on your device.

Cloud account and sync

When cloud features are enabled, Czechify creates an anonymous Supabase account and synchronizes selected learning progress. You may optionally link an email address to preserve your account across devices.

AI features

AI tutor messages and writing/grammar requests are sent over HTTPS through a Czechify server to DeepSeek. Do not include sensitive personal information in AI prompts.

Speech and microphone

Microphone access is requested only for pronunciation practice. Device speech recognition is used by default. If you explicitly enable optional cloud pronunciation, a short recording is sent over HTTPS through Czechify to OpenAI Whisper for transcription. Czechify does not intentionally retain the recording after processing.

Analytics and advertising

Czechify does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics, or a third-party crash-reporting SDK.

Your choices and rights

You can export or delete cloud and local learner data in Settings → Account & data. If you cannot access the app, use the account-deletion instructions.

Contact

For questions, contact the maintainer through the Czechify GitHub repository.
