Czechify Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Version: 2026-08-30.1

1. Controller and contact

Czechify is operated by Mahesh Pathak, Příčná 1892/4, Nové Město, 110 00 Praha, Czechia (the “controller”, “we”, or “us”).

For privacy questions or requests, email email.czechify@gmail.com. No data protection officer has been appointed because Czechify's current processing does not require one.

2. Summary

Czechify automatically creates an anonymous cloud account and synchronises learning data to it. You may optionally connect Google or add an email address for account recovery and multi-device use. The optional learner profile (name, level, study goal, focus, pace, teacher and reminder preference), placement, and learning progress synchronise so a recovered account is ready on another device. AI chat history, exam results, device settings, and downloaded lesson audio stay on your device. AI tutor text and optional cloud-pronunciation audio leave the device as described below.

There are no ads, third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

4. What stays on the device

AI conversation history, exam results, visual/device settings, notification permission and scheduled notification identifiers, device consent history, and downloaded lesson audio are stored locally. The optional learner profile, reminder preference, placement, and learning progress also have a local copy but the categories listed as synced above are sent to the cloud. Authentication tokens are stored using Android Keystore or Apple Keychain facilities. Android app backup is disabled.

Your device's default speech-recognition feature may process audio on the device or send it to the operating-system provider (for example, Google or Apple), depending on your device and its settings. That processing is controlled by the provider's terms and privacy notice, not by Czechify.

5. Account and cloud sync

On first use, Czechify creates a random anonymous Supabase user ID. No email, password, or name is required. Synced rows are tied to that account ID and an installation ID. You may later link an Google account or an email address and password to recover the same account or use it on another device.

Deleting an account removes the old account and its synced rows. If you continue using the app, Czechify then creates a new, empty anonymous account so cloud-backed functions can keep working.

7. International transfers

Czechify's primary Supabase project data is hosted in Paris, France, within the European Union. Supabase's Data Processing Addendum incorporates the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where they are required for restricted transfers or onward transfers.

OpenAI's Data Processing Addendum uses applicable adequacy decisions and Standard Contractual Clauses for restricted transfers and onward transfers. You may ask us for information about those safeguards.

Scaleway states that models on its Generative APIs infrastructure operate in Europe without sending prompts or outputs to the model creator or another third-party model service. This AI-text flow therefore does not itself require a transfer outside the EEA. See Scaleway's Generative APIs data-privacy information.

8. Pronunciation and consent

Cloud speech is off until you allow it. When enabled, a short recording is sent over encrypted connections through a Supabase Edge Function to OpenAI, and the transcript returns to the app. Czechify does not write the recording or transcript to its cloud database and removes its temporary local recording after the attempt.

OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default and may be retained for abuse monitoring for up to 30 days unless a zero-data-retention configuration applies. You can switch cloud speech off at any time in Settings. Withdrawal affects future recordings and does not make earlier consent-based processing unlawful. The app keeps a local record of the consent/withdrawal time and notice version until app data is cleared.

9. AI tutor and reports

AI output can be inaccurate, inappropriate, or offensive and is not professional advice. It does not make legal or similarly significant decisions about you. Your full chat history stays on your device, but the current message, recent context, and sometimes a generated summary are sent for each request.

Reporting a tutor reply opens your email app with the tutor output, scenario, time, reason, and optional note. Your side of the conversation is not included unless you put it in the note. Your email provider may add your name, address, signature, or other account details.

10. Retention

  • Local learner data: until you delete the account/data in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall. Downloaded lesson audio remains after in-app account deletion because it is non-personal course content; use Settings → Downloads → Clear audio cache, clear app storage, or uninstall to remove it.
  • Cloud account and synced learning data: until account deletion. Unlinked anonymous accounts and their synced data are scheduled for deletion after 90 days without use.
  • Supabase backups and security logs: deleted records may remain in isolated, time-limited backups or security logs until the provider's plan-based retention window expires. They are not restored as active user records except for disaster recovery.
  • Cloud pronunciation: Czechify does not persist recordings in its cloud database. OpenAI may retain API data for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring unless zero data retention applies.
  • Scaleway AI input/output: Czechify does not store it in its cloud database. Scaleway's default Zero Data Retention policy says prompts and outputs are not collected, read, reused, or used for training. Anonymised operational usage metadata may be retained for up to six months. In rare error, security, harmful-content, or misuse cases, relevant request content may be stored temporarily for investigation for up to two weeks.
  • Local consent history: until account/data deletion, app-data clearing, or uninstall.
  • AI reports and ordinary support correspondence: while the issue is handled and normally no longer than 12 months afterwards. Minimal records needed to prove completion of a privacy request may be kept for up to three years, unless a legal claim or law requires longer.

11. Security

Czechify uses HTTPS/TLS, owner-scoped database row-level security, authenticated Edge Functions, server-side provider secrets, rate limits, and secure operating-system storage for authentication sessions. Access is limited to what is needed to operate and support the service. No internet service can promise absolute security; contact us if you suspect a problem.

12. Your rights

Subject to the GDPR's conditions and exceptions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Export and in-app deletion are under Settings → Account & data. For an external deletion request, follow the Czechify account-deletion instructions. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. We normally respond within one month.

You may complain to the Czech supervisory authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, posta@uoou.cz, or to the competent authority where you live or work.

13. Children

Czechify is not directed to children under 16 and should be listed in Google Play for an audience aged 16 and over. Czechify does not collect a birth date. A person under 16 must not enable cloud speech or submit personal data to an AI feature. If you believe a child has done so, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

14. Changes

We will update the date and version when this notice changes. If a change affects a consent you previously gave, Czechify will ask again before further consent-based processing.
