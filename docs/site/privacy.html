The short version

Czechify never asks for your name, email address, or any other personal detail in order to work. Your lessons, progress, and chat history live on your device. Your progress is backed up to an anonymous account so a lost phone does not cost you your learning.

There is no advertising in this app, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your name, learning progress, streaks, badges, review schedule, exam results, your chat history with the AI tutor, downloaded audio, and your settings are held in a local database on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Your account

Czechify creates an account for you automatically the first time you open it. That account is anonymous: it asks for no email address, no password, and no name. Its only purpose is to hold your progress so it is not lost.

You may later add an email address and password so the same account works on another device. That is the only point at which Czechify holds an email address, it is entirely your choice, and everything you have already learned carries across.

Transfers outside the EEA

The United Kingdom is covered by a European Commission adequacy decision. Transfers to OpenAI in the United States and to DeepSeek are made on the basis of the safeguards described in our contracts with those providers; you can ask us for details. Cloud pronunciation is additionally sent only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Pronunciation and your voice

Pronunciation practice records short clips of your speech. By default Czechify uses the speech-recognition service built into your device, and your operating-system provider controls how that service handles speech.

Nothing is sent to our cloud speech service unless you switch it on in Settings. If you do, the recording is relayed through our server to OpenAI and a transcript comes back. The recording is not stored on our servers afterwards. Withdrawing is as easy as giving it and takes effect immediately; when you do agree, we record when you agreed and to which version of this notice, so both of us can check later what was actually agreed.

AI tutor

Your conversation history stays on your device. Please do not include sensitive personal information in messages to the tutor.

The tutor is a language model and can occasionally produce something wrong, inappropriate, or offensive. Every tutor reply in the app carries a flag you can tap to report it. A report sends us the tutor's own words and the scenario — not your side of the conversation, unless you choose to describe it yourself.

How long we keep things

  • On your device: until you delete the data in the app or uninstall it.
  • In your cloud account: until you delete your account.
  • Anonymous accounts you never linked to an email: automatically deleted after 90 days without use, together with everything synced to them.
  • Voice recordings: not retained after the transcript is returned.
  • Consent records: kept while your account exists, because they are the evidence that consent was given.

Our providers may keep operational backups and security logs for their own retention periods.

Analytics and advertising

Czechify contains no advertising, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, no analytics SDK, and no third-party crash-reporting SDK.

Children

Czechify is not directed at children under 16. Czechify does not know your age, so the cloud pronunciation control asks you to confirm eligibility. Anyone under 16 should use that feature only with permission from a parent or guardian.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable form, and withdraw any consent you have given.

Export and deletion are built into the app under Settings → Account & data; deletion there removes both the local and the cloud copy. If you cannot open the app, use the account-deletion instructions. For anything else, email email.czechify@gmail.com.

You may also complain to the Czech supervisory authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, posta@uoou.cz), or to the authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what you have agreed to, you will be asked again rather than opted in silently.
