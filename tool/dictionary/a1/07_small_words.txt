# A1 dictionary — adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions and small words.

@ proč

# prepositions

@ v
pos: prep
en: in; at; on (days)
rel: inside; within
case: + 6 for a place (v Praze); + 4 for days (v pondělí)
forms: ve

@ na
pos: prep
en: on; at; to; for
rel: onto; at
case: + 6 for where (na poště); + 4 for where to (na poštu)

@ do
pos: prep
en: to; into; until
rel: into; till
case: + 2 (do školy, do pěti)

@ z
pos: prep
en: from; out of
rel: origin; out
case: + 2 (z Prahy)
forms: ze

@ s
pos: prep
en: with
rel: together; and
case: + 7 (s mlékem)
forms: se

@ u
pos: prep
en: at; by; near; at someone's
rel: next to; near
case: + 2 (u nádraží)

@ od
pos: prep
en: from; since
rel: away from
case: + 2 (od pěti)

@ k
pos: prep
en: to; towards
rel: toward
case: + 3 (k obědu)
forms: ke

@ o
pos: prep
en: about; at (the weekend)
rel: about; concerning
case: + 6 (o víkendu)

@ po
pos: prep
en: after
rel: later; past
case: + 6 (po práci)

@ pro
pos: prep
en: for
ex: To je pro tebe. = That is for you.
rel: for; on behalf of
case: + 4 (pro tebe)

@ před
pos: prep
en: in front of; before
forms: přede
rel: earlier; ago
case: + 7 (před školou)

@ za
pos: prep
en: for; in (time from now); behind
rel: after; behind
case: + 4 (za týden = in a week); + 7 (za domem = behind the house)

@ vedle
pos: prep
en: next to; beside
rel: near; by
case: + 2 (vedle banky)

@ naproti
pos: prep
en: opposite; across from
rel: facing; across
case: + 3 (naproti parku)

@ bez
pos: prep
en: without
ex: Kávu bez cukru, prosím. = Coffee without sugar, please.
rel: none
case: + 2 (bez cukru)

# conjunctions

@ a
pos: conj
en: and
rel: plus; also

@ ale
pos: conj
en: but
rel: however

@ nebo
pos: conj
en: or
rel: either

@ protože
pos: conj
en: because
ex: Nepřijdu, protože jsem nemocný. = I am not coming because I am ill.
rel: reason; why

@ proto
pos: conj
en: so; that's why
ex: Mám hlad, proto jdu na oběd. = I am hungry, so I am going to lunch.
rel: therefore

@ tak
pos: conj
en: so; then
rel: therefore; in that case

@ i
pos: conj
en: and; also; even
ex: Mluvím česky i anglicky. = I speak both Czech and English.
rel: too; both

@ jako
pos: conj
en: as; like
rel: similar

@ aby
pos: conj
en: so that; in order to
forms: abych, abys, abychom, abyste
ex: Učím se česky, abych rozuměl lidem. = I learn Czech so that I understand people.
rel: purpose

@ jestli
pos: conj
en: if; whether
rel: question

@ když
pos: conj
en: when; if
ex: Když prší, jsem doma. = When it rains, I am at home.
rel: time; condition

@ by
pos: part
en: would
rel: conditional; polite
table: Forms |
row: já | bych
row: ty | bys
row: on / ona | by
row: my | bychom
row: vy | byste
row: oni | by
note: Rád bych… = I would like…
