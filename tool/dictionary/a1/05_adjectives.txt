@ rád
pos: adj
en: glad; happy to; (with mít) to like
rel: like; enjoy; love; glad
table: Forms |
row: man | rád
row: woman | ráda
row: thing (neuter) | rádo
row: men or mixed group | rádi
row: women | rády
key: man / woman = rád / ráda
note: Mám rád kávu. = I like coffee (said by a man); a woman says Mám ráda kávu. Rád čtu. = I like reading.
