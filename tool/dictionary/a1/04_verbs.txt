# A1 dictionary — verbs.
# pres: já, ty, on/ona/ono, my, vy, oni. Where two forms are both used,
# the everyday one comes first (piju/piji). For a perfective verb (pf) the
# present-tense forms mean the future: zaplatím = I will pay.
